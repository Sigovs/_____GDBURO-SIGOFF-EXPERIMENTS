About us

A showroom that reads like a catalogue.

Meridian has bought and sold sport and exotic cars from one building on the north side since 2009. Every car is photographed on the same sweep, measured against the same specification sheet, and listed with its history attached rather than summarised.

We keep a small inventory on purpose. If a car is here, someone on the floor has driven it.
