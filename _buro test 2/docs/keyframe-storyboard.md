# KEYFRAME STORYBOARD — _buro test 2

Five frozen frames and the four transitions between them. Written 2026-09-04, before
any markup.

**The test every frame has to pass:** *stopped, with nothing moving and no context, is
this a composed photograph of a page?* A frame that only works as part of a sequence is
not a keyframe, it is a tween (`MJ4`).

**The compositional spine.** The five frames swing across the format rather than
settling in the middle: **right → centre-left → centre-left → lower-left →
centre-right.** Nothing is centred, and the record column, once established at Frame C,
holds its position for the rest of the page. Colour tokens and spans are from
[visual-direction.md](visual-direction.md).

## FRAME A — 00 / ENTRY

*Outside. Night. Exclusion, and one invitation.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dominant mass** | The perimeter wall and gatehouse, occupying the **right 45%** of the frame, running out past the right edge. One lit aperture within it, at roughly the upper third of that mass. Full bleed. |
| **Secondary mass** | The identity block, low left: wordmark, then *121 private suites*, then *Lake Zurich, Illinois*. Three ranks, one column, flush left. |
| **Text position** | **Low left**, in `text` (cols 2–7), sitting in the darkest quiet region of the photograph. The persistent header — wordmark hairline, act number `00`, TOUR, phone — runs along the top edge at `--ink-70`. |
| **Negative space** | **~50% of the frame: the unlit wall, upper left.** It is active, and it means *the rest of the compound, which you are not being shown.* This is the frame's argument, not its margin. |
| **Plan position** | **A single hairline along the bottom edge**, `--rule-construction`, running the full width and continuing past both edges. Unlabelled. It reads as a kerb, a threshold or a plinth — as architecture, not as interface. |
| **Image / architecture** | Full bleed, level, tripod height. The wall recedes to the right; the gate is closed. No car anywhere in the frame. |
| **Focal point** | **The lit aperture.** It is the only warm-adjacent value in the frame and the brightest pixel on the page so far. |
| **Eye path** | lit aperture → down and left across the dark wall → the wordmark → down the three ranks → arrives at the bottom hairline and runs right along it, off frame. |
| **FG / MID / BG** | **FG** the bottom hairline and the header, both hairline weight · **MID** the wall and gate, the frame's whole body · **BG** the sky, near `--void`, with no stars and no gradient — a lid, not a backdrop. |
| **Survives from previous** | Nothing — this is the arrival. |
| **Prepares next** | The **bottom hairline**, which will climb the left margin and become the rail. And the **gate**, which is the aperture that Frame B is behind. |

**Why it holds frozen:** one dominant, one focal point, one direction, and the largest
mass on screen is empty on purpose. It identifies what the page is in three lines
without a paragraph (`C18`).

## FRAME B — 01 / ARRIVAL

*Inside the gate. The street of doors. Scale.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dominant mass** | The internal frontage: a lit roadway with rows of closed sectional doors on both sides, receding to a deep point **left of centre**. The page's single monumental mass, and the only one (`L5`). Full bleed. |
| **Secondary mass** | The door rhythm itself — a regular beat of vertical divisions on both sides, which is the mechanical rhythm the Anchor asks for, delivered by the architecture rather than by the layout. |
| **Text position** | **Upper right**, one mono caption of ≤10 words in the `record` span, in the dark above the right-hand roofline. It carries one verified figure and its source. Nothing else. |
| **Negative space** | **The upper third — the dark ceiling plane and the roofline.** It means *the building continues above you*, which is the frame's scale argument. It is the second-largest mass and it is empty. |
| **Plan position** | **The left margin, `rail` (col 1).** Over this act the hairline from Frame A has climbed and gained a second line, a perpendicular, and two more — by the frozen frame it is legibly a fragment of a plan. A marker sits on it. Still unlabelled. |
| **Image / architecture** | Full bleed, level, tripod height, the camera standing in the roadway. One key direction — overhead LED, cool, running down the length. |
| **Focal point** | **The deep point of the corridor**, left of centre, where the two door rows converge and the light concentrates. |
| **Eye path** | enters bottom-left along the roadway → runs to the deep point → lifts to the right roofline → the caption, upper right → drops back left down the rail. **The path is a closed loop**, which is what makes a frame with almost no text hold for a viewport and a half. |
| **FG / MID / BG** | **FG** the rail and the near door edges cropping past the frame · **MID** the roadway and the two door rows · **BG** the deep point, where detail is lost to distance rather than to darkness. |
| **Survives from previous** | The hairline, now a drawing. The header, unchanged. The one-key-direction lighting. |
| **Prepares next** | The **left-of-centre focal position**, which Frame C keeps. The **rail**, which Frame C expands. And the door rhythm, which is the plan's bay rhythm seen from inside. |

**Why it holds frozen:** a one-point recession with the focal point off-axis and
counterweighted by the caption diagonally opposite — optical balance without symmetry
(`C5`), and a directed eye path that resolves (`C6`, `C11`).

## FRAME C — 02 / THE COMPOUND, expanded

*The recognition. The line was this place.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dominant mass** | **The compound, drawn.** Eleven suite buildings as shallow-extruded masses on a fixed ~15° axonometric axis, the clubhouse as a distinct mass, the roadway as a near-planar lighter field. Occupies `stage` (cols 2–8), reading **centre-left**, extending past the left edge so the compound is larger than the frame. |
| **Secondary mass** | **The record column** (cols 9–12), established here for the first time and held for the rest of the page: the count line — *121 SUITES · 11 BUILDINGS* — the legend, and the availability figure with its capture date. |
| **Text position** | **Right, in `record`.** Flush left within its span, on `--deck`, mono keys and grotesque values. The header's act number reads `02`. |
| **Negative space** | **The unbuilt land around the compound**, ~30% of the stage, at `--deck`. It means *the boundary* — the reason the word "gated" is in the proposition. It is drawn as absence, with the perimeter as the only line in it. |
| **Plan position** | **The plan IS the frame.** This is the payoff: the object that has been a marginal hairline for two acts is now the subject at full scale, and nothing announces it. |
| **Image / architecture** | **No photography in this frame.** The register is surveyed; the ground is `--deck`; the light is even and low with one key direction across every mass. This is one of only two acts with no photograph in it, and the absence is the register change. |
| **Focal point** | **The compound's entry road**, where the roadway meets the perimeter — the same threshold Frame A's gate was, now seen in plan. The eye is given the way in before it is given the inventory. |
| **Eye path** | enters at the entry road, lower left → travels up the roadway through the building masses → the clubhouse, which is distinct → right to the count line → down the legend → back left into the drawing. |
| **FG / MID / BG** | **FG** the record column and the perimeter line, both at hairline weight · **MID** the building masses, all at `--mass`, separated by line rather than by value · **BG** the `--deck` ground, flat, with no gradient and no vignette. |
| **Survives from previous** | The **rail's own geometry** — this is that object, enlarged, not a replacement for it. The left-of-centre focal position. The header. The one key direction. |
| **Prepares next** | The **record column**, now permanent. The bay rhythm inside the masses, about to be revealed. And the entry road, which is where the eye already is when Frame D lights one bay. |

**Why it holds frozen:** it is a complete, legible architectural drawing with a
count, a legend and a boundary. Frozen at any point it is a plan someone could read —
which is the whole test of `MJ4` for a scrubbed sequence.

## FRAME D — 02 / THE COMPOUND, selected subject

*One bay, illuminated. Specificity.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dominant mass** | **One illuminated bay**, `--bay-lit`, inside one focused building — positioned **lower-left of the stage**, with light spilling onto the roadway in front of its door. It measures **3.91:1 against the unlit mass and 4.45:1 against the ground**, so it is unambiguously the dominant with no border, no outline and no marker. |
| **Secondary mass** | **The focused building** at `--mass-lit`, its bay subdivisions now drawn at `--rule-partition`. Everything else in the compound has fallen to `--mass` and lost its subdivisions — subordination by light, never by an overlay. |
| **Text position** | **Right, in `record`**, now at its densest: `TYPE A` · `30′ × 50′` · `+ MEZZANINE 30′ × 31′` · `2,430 SQ FT` · `≈6 CARS, 3 MOTORCYCLES` · `FROM $699,000`. Mono keys, tabular values, hairline rules between rows, no box. A hairline leader runs from the plate's first row to the lit bay and stays anchored. |
| **Negative space** | **The darkened remainder of the compound** — the 120 rooms that are not this one. It is the largest mass in the frame and it is the frame's argument: *you have chosen, and the choosing had scale.* |
| **Plan position** | Still the full stage, reframed by roughly 25% on the focused building. **The axis has not changed** — it is a reframe, not a fly-to. |
| **Image / architecture** | Still no photography. Still `--deck`. The only value change since Frame C is light. |
| **Focal point** | **The lit bay.** Unambiguous, and arrived at by luminance rather than by a UI affordance. |
| **Eye path** | the lit bay → the light spill on the roadway in front of it → the leader line, right → the spec plate's first row → down the figures → back along the leader to the bay. **A tight loop between the object and its record**, which is the act's entire proposition. |
| **FG / MID / BG** | **FG** the leader line and the record · **MID** the focused building and its lit bay · **BG** the subordinated compound, present and legible, at `--mass`. |
| **Survives from previous** | The drawing, the axis, the ground, the record column's position, the header. Nothing has been replaced — only lit and reframed. |
| **Prepares next** | **The lit bay's footprint**, which is about to enlarge and become the frame Frame E is seen through. Its position lower-left gives it room to grow up and right into the viewport. |

**Why it holds frozen:** one dominant established by light, a subordinated field that
stays legible, and a record tied to the subject by a line. A stopped frame here is an
architectural drawing with one room called out — a completely conventional and
completely readable artefact.

## FRAME E — 03 / THE SUITE · the visual peak

*Inside. The drawing was a description of this.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dominant mass** | **The interior of the suite**, from the doorway, at tripod height, full bleed. The deep point sits **centre-right** — the page's spatial swing completes here, having run right → centre-left → centre-left → lower-left → centre-right. |
| **Secondary mass** | **The mezzanine**, reading as a horizontal band across the upper third with the wood staircase rising to it at the right. It is the second thing the eye finds and it is what makes the volume read as two levels rather than as a tall box. |
| **Text position** | **Display line upper left**, four words or fewer, at `--t-display` — one of only two moments of typographic scale on the page. **Spec plate right**, in `record` (cols 9–12), continuing exactly where Frames C and D put it. |
| **Negative space** | **The unlit rear of the room and the soffit under the mezzanine** — roughly 35% of the frame, and the most important empty space on the site. It is *the volume being sold.* An interior photograph whose dark end is crushed to black has thrown this away (`IM2`). |
| **Plan position** | **A small footprint notation in the record**, ~40mm, hairline, with the selected bay marked — the drawing that just became this room, kept where the reader can check it against what they are looking at. |
| **Image / architecture** | Full bleed, level, tripod height, **locked camera**. Insulated walls and ceiling, sealed floor, commercial overhead door up, pedestrian door, wood staircase. One key direction: the overhead LED, cool, plus daylight through the open door. |
| **Focal point** | **The lit floor plane in the middle distance**, where the door light lands. Not the door, not the staircase — the empty floor, because the empty floor is the product. |
| **Eye path** | the lit floor → back to the deep point, centre-right → up to the mezzanine band → left along it to the display line, upper left → down the right side to the spec plate → the footprint notation → back into the room. |
| **FG / MID / BG** | **FG** the door reveal cropping the frame on both sides — the visitor is standing *in* the opening · **MID** the floor plane and the volume · **BG** the rear wall and the soffit, dark, with detail retained. |
| **Survives from previous** | **The footprint**, which is now the frame's own opening. The record column's position and its type. The header. And the selected suite's identity — type and dimensions — carried forward from Frame D. |
| **Prepares next** | The **door seal at the bottom of the frame**, which is Act 04's macro subject, and the shutter that will drop to a slot across it. |

**Why it holds frozen:** the page's most composed frame — a real room, one dominant,
active negative space carrying the proposition, a display line and a plate in the
positions the previous two frames established. This is the frame the site is judged on.

# TRANSITIONS

Four seams. Motion character, not production timing — durations live in
[motion-dna.md §9](motion-dna.md).

## A → B · through the gate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Remains anchored** | The header. The bottom hairline. The one-key-direction lighting. |
| **Moves first** | The gate leaf, sliding laterally across the frame. It is the only thing moving for the first half. |
| **Moves second** | Frame B's roadway, revealed behind it — already composed, already lit, not fading in. |
| **Arrives late** | The caption, upper right, by opacity after the leaf has cleared. **Text never arrives during travel.** |
| **Transforms** | The bottom hairline begins climbing the left margin — slowly, and it is the only element that survives the cut visibly changed. |
| **Disappears** | Frame A's identity block and its wall, occluded by the leaf. Not dissolved. |
| **Scrubbed / triggered** | **Scrubbed**, `scrub: 0.6`. The visitor opens the gate. |
| **Pinning** | **No.** Neither act is pinned. |
| **Flip** | **No.** Nothing changes layout; one layer occludes another. |
| **Masking** | **Yes** — the leaf is a moving mask edge, which is what makes it read as a physical door rather than as a wipe. |
| **Three.js** | **No.** Two photographic plates and a moving edge. |
| **Character** | Heavy and lateral. Slow to break, continuous, a long settle. A gate that weighs something. |

## B → C · the register change

| | |
|---|---|
| **Remains anchored** | The rail's geometry — **the single most important anchor in the page**, because it is what makes the recognition land. The header. The left-of-centre focal position. |
| **Moves first** | Nothing moves. **The light goes flat** — a grade change from photographic dusk to `--deck`, with the roadway's key direction dropping out. This is the `interruption` shot: the seam is the event. |
| **Moves second** | The rail's `viewBox`, tweening from a one-column crop to the compound's full extent. This is the camera, and it is the only movement for most of the transition. |
| **Arrives late** | Building masses extruding, staggered 24ms from the entry road outward, capped at eight. Then — last — the count line and the legend, by opacity. |
| **Transforms** | The rail becomes the compound. Same object, same path data, enlarged. **It is never swapped for a look-alike**; if it were, the visitor would feel the seam without being able to name it. |
| **Disappears** | Frame B's photograph, by grade change rather than by fade. |
| **Scrubbed / triggered** | **Scrubbed**, `scrub: 0.6`. |
| **Pinning** | **Yes** — this is the front of act 02's 2.6vh pin. |
| **Flip** | **No.** One element's own `viewBox` and geometry; a direct tween owns it more cheaply and more precisely (`DNA45`). |
| **Masking** | **No.** |
| **Three.js** | **No** — and this is the transition where it would be most tempting. A `viewBox` tween on a fixed axis *is* the camera move this needs, and the drawing has to exist anyway as the fallback. |
| **Character** | A drawing being unrolled on a table. Even, unhurried, no acceleration into the final frame. The recognition must arrive quietly or it becomes a reveal effect. |

## C → D · SELECT

| | |
|---|---|
| **Remains anchored** | The compound's full extent and **its axis, which never changes**. The record column. The ground. |
| **Moves first** | **Light, and nothing else.** The focused building's faces lift to `--mass-lit`; its neighbours fall to `--mass`. Nothing translates yet — which is precisely what makes the change read as *illumination* rather than as *navigation*. |
| **Moves second** | The `viewBox` eases in ~25% on the focused mass. **A reframe, not a zoom-to-fill** — a map that flies to a building is a game map. |
| **Arrives late** | Bay subdivisions inside the focused mass, drawn from the party wall outward at `--rule-partition`. Then the record's new rows. Then the leader line, last, connecting them. |
| **Transforms** | On bay selection: one bay's interior face lifts to `--bay-lit` and light spills onto the roadway. The activation is *physical* — a surface with light on it, not a surface emitting light. |
| **Disappears** | Nothing. Subordinated masses lose light, not presence. |
| **Scrubbed / triggered** | **Triggered**, 320ms, `--ease-enter`. Feedback must answer the input immediately; binding selection to scroll would take the choice away from the visitor. |
| **Pinning** | **Yes** — still inside act 02's pin. |
| **Flip** | **Yes — on the record column only.** The rows change count and content between the three levels, and Flip animates a layout change nobody had to hand-author. **Never on the drawing.** |
| **Masking** | **No.** |
| **Three.js** | **No.** Subordination is a value change on eleven masses. |
| **Character** | A hand moving a lamp across a physical model. Immediate, precise, mechanical — no easing drama, because this is an instrument responding. |

## D → E · ENTER

**The one moment where the drawing becomes the photograph. The signature is judged
here.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Remains anchored** | **The selected footprint's centre.** It is the fixed point the whole movement is composed around and it does not drift by a pixel. |
| **Moves first** | **The rest of the compound**, outward and down in luminance. The surroundings leave *before* the subject moves, so the eye is already on the footprint when it starts. |
| **Moves second** | The footprint enlarges along its own axis, scrubbed, until its inner edge reaches the frame. **It stays a drawing the whole way** — hairline weight, no fill, no premature photograph inside it. |
| **Arrives late** | The hairline outline dissolves **last, and alone**, over `--dur-settle`. The drawing is the final thing to leave, which is what makes it read as having *become* the room rather than as having been replaced by it. |
| **Transforms** | At the moment the footprint's inner edge passes the viewport, it becomes a **`clip-path` on the photographic layer**. The room is already loaded and already composed behind it — the footprint is not revealing a transition, it is revealing a frame that was always there. |
| **Disappears** | The compound, the legend, the count line, the surveyed ground. |
| **Scrubbed / triggered** | **Scrubbed.** The visitor walks through their own door. |
| **Pinning** | **The hand-off between act 02's pin and act 03's.** Only one is ever active (`DNA47`); 02 releases as the movement completes and 03 takes hold on the composed frame. Tested specifically at fast scroll and at reverse. |
| **Flip** | **No — and this is the interesting rejection.** Flip is the reflex answer for *"an element becomes a bigger element"* and it is wrong here: Flip interpolates a box, and what must interpolate is a **clip region on a different layer**. Flip would move the drawing to the photograph's position; it would not make the drawing *be* the aperture. |
| **Masking** | **Yes — this is the technique.** `clip-path` driven from the same path data the drawing uses, so the mask is not an approximation of the footprint, it **is** the footprint (`NEVER 30`). |
| **Three.js** | **No, and it would make this harder.** From WebGL the footprint must be projected to screen space and handed to the DOM before the mask can exist. The 2D path already lives in the coordinate space the mask needs. |
| **Character** | Inevitable. Slow to break, continuous through, and it **resolves** rather than arrives. The failure mode to avoid is a flourish: this should feel like a door being walked through, not like a transition being performed. |

## What no transition does

- **Nothing moves on one beat.** Every seam has at least three phases, and text is
  always in the last one.
- **No cross-fade between acts.** Occlusion, grade change or mask — never a dissolve
  through a blurred middle state (`O5`).
- **No transition is longer than the content behind it takes to load.** A seam is never
  used to hide a pending asset (`DNA66`).
- **Every 25 / 50 / 75% frame of all four is legible and identifiable** (`MJ4`). This is
  checked by stopping, not by watching.
