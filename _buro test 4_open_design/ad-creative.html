D · Promotion

The prize, with its paperwork

Highest attention of the four, and the highest compliance load. Sweepstakes creative carries platform rules that sit outside design — this one should not go live until the items flagged below are cleared.

Primary text

The first 50 buyers at Luxe Corsa are entered to win a VIP trip to the 2026 Monaco Grand Prix. 121 suites, 75 available.

Headline

Required disclosure

First 50 eligible purchases automatically entered. Void where prohibited by law. Open to residents 18+ or older. Prize: Trip to 2026 Monaco Grand Prix; approximate retail value $100,000. Odds of winning depend on eligible entries. See official rules for details.

Do not launch without clearing: official rules hosted at a public URL; a statement that the promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by the platform; sponsor name and address; entry period start and end dates; and state registration or bonding where the prize value requires it. Prize ARV is stated as $100,000, which is above the threshold that triggers registration in some US states.
